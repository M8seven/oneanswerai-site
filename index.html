How it works

Ask once.

This is what Meta-Fusion does with a single question. Scroll to run it.

Your question

How much caffeine is in an espresso?

  1. First model

    A single espresso, about 30 ml, has roughly 63 mg of caffeine. A double has twice that. Most adults are advised to stay under 400 mg a day.

  2. Second model

    One shot of espresso carries around 75 mg of caffeine, depending on the beans and the machine. The usual limit for adults is 400 mg a day.

  3. Third model

    Expect about 63 mg in a 30 ml shot, a little more or less with the roast. Healthy adults should keep below 400 mg a day.

Meta-Fusion · one answer

A single espresso is about 30 ml. It has about 63 mg of caffeine. Adults are advised to stay under 400 mg a day.

In the app

What using it actually looks like.

01Home

It opens on a text field.

There is one box. You type the question or tap a suggestion. The attachment button takes a PDF or a photo.

02Chat

You read one answer.

With Pro, the question goes to all three families and you get the merged text back. On Free, Google Gemini answers, except your very first question, which is fused from all three.

03Images

Edit a photo by describing the change.

Send a picture, write what should be different, get the new picture back. Here the prompt was "Make it Sunny".

A count of what you asked.

Questions, streak and active days for the week, and how many answers came from each provider.

When they disagree

When they disagree, you see it.

Any language model can state something false in a calm voice. Asking three does not make that go away.

OneAnswerAI does not eliminate wrong answers. With Pro, a panel under the fused answer lists where the models disagreed, so you know which point to check before you rely on it.

The app compares the three answers and flags where their claims differ.

The three families

Three families. No version numbers.

The app talks to a family, not to one fixed model. When we move the family to a provider's newer model, you get it without updating the app.

  1. OpenAI

    Pro

    The generalist.

    Handles reasoning, code and structured tasks. Available with Pro, in Meta-Fusion or on its own in Picking Mode.

  2. Google Gemini

    Free and Pro

    The one that answers on Free.

    Quick, and good with images. Your very first Free question is fused from all three; after that, every Free question comes here. With Pro it is one of the three.

  3. Anthropic Claude

    Pro

    The careful reader.

    Suited to long documents and questions that need slow reasoning. Available with Pro.

In Meta-Fusion all three answer in parallel. You do not wait for one to finish before the next starts.
